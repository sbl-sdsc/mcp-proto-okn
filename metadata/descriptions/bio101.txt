KB Bio 101 (bio101) is the 2013 OWL2 export of a large knowledge base that biologists and knowledge engineers built from an introductory biology textbook for SRI's AURA system under Vulcan Inc.'s Project Halo. It covers chemistry, molecular biology, cell biology, physiology, genetics, evolution and ecology in about 6,300 named concepts and roughly 1.06 million triples. It is an ontology, not instance data: every concept, such as Mitochondrion, Glycolysis or Ribosome, is an owl:Class with a short definition in aura:user-description. Relations come from the UT Austin Component Library, for example has-part, is-inside, agent, object, raw-material, result, subevent, site and has-function. They appear only inside owl:Restriction axioms reached through rdfs:subClassOf and nested owl:intersectionOf lists, never as direct triples. Shared nodes in a concept's graph are marked with aura:ID-N classes. The graph answers textbook questions such as what a structure is made of and where it sits, which steps, inputs, products and locations make up a biological process, what an entity's function is, and how concepts specialise one another. It uses no external identifiers.
