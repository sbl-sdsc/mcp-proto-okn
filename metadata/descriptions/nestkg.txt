The NeST Knowledge Graph (nestkg) is an RDF representation of the Nested Systems in Tumors (NeST) cancer systems map, published by NDEx and derived from a multiscale analysis of somatic mutations across 13 cancer types. It contains 395 protein systems arranged in a nested part-of hierarchy that spans scales from small protein complexes to broad cellular processes, each carrying a system name, a member count, and the HiSig weight and Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted p-value that quantify how much of the observed mutational signal the model attributes to it. Systems link to their member proteins by UniProt accession, and 701 reified associations connect a system to one of fourteen MONDO cancer terms, thirteen tumor types plus a pan-cancer aggregate, most of them carrying the fraction of tumors in that cohort with a mutated member. The underlying Integrated Association Stringency network supplies 209,956 protein-protein interactions, each reified so it carries its IAS score. The graph answers which protein systems are recurrently mutated in a cancer type and how those systems nest.
