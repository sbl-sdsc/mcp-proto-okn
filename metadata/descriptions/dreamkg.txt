DREAM-KG (Dynamic, REsponsive, Adaptive, and Multifaceted Knowledge Graph) is an Open Knowledge Network addressing homelessness for case workers, service providers, law enforcement, nonprofits, and people experiencing homelessness. The release loaded in the FRINK federation describes 1,321 social services, structured with Schema.org vocabulary: each service has a name, description, eligibility text, area served, service channels with a phone number, website and location, and weekday opening hours given as text. Services are classified by about 500 category codes covering service type, target audience (for example adults, people with low income, the uninsured), language, cost and availability. The 1,018 service locations, mostly in California and Pennsylvania, carry an address, latitude and longitude, a GeoSPARQL point geometry and a link to their KnowWhereGraph ZIP code area, which is the join key to other spatial graphs; language codes link to Lexvo. The graph also holds 3,762 user reviews with text, although this release does not link a review to the service it is about. It contains about 95,000 triples and no longer carries PROV provenance records.
