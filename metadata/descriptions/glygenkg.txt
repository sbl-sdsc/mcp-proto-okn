GlyGen KG (glygenkg) is an RDF knowledge graph built from GlyGen, a data integration project for carbohydrate and glycoconjugate data, and covers protein glycosylation in human. It holds 8,112 human glycoproteins from UniProtKB with their names, genes, Ensembl gene loci and chromosome coordinates, and about 74,700 glycosylation records. Each record links a protein to a glycosylation site (residue position and amino acid), to the glycan attached there when it is known (1,248 glycans identified by GlyTouCan accession and labelled with composition and N-linked or O-linked type), and to the PubMed publication that reports it. A smaller layer annotates protein residues with single-nucleotide variants that create or remove glycosylation sequons or sites, flagged as germline, somatic, cancer or disease variants, and with phosphorylation sites and their kinases. The graph answers which proteins and residues are glycosylated, which glycans occur at a site or across proteins, which publications support a glycosylation site, and which variants in a gene are predicted to gain or lose glycosylation.
