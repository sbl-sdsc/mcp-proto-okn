The Safe Agricultural Products and Water Graph (SAWGraph) is an open knowledge network for environmental health researchers, regulatory agencies, and public health officials tracking per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) and other contaminants in food and water systems. It uses the ContaminOSO ontology (coso:) to model sampled features (wells, water bodies, facilities, fields), environmental media (groundwater, surface water, waste water, soil, sediment, animal or plant tissue), sample locations (Level 13 S2 cells and Level 3 administrative regions), sample material types drawn from FOODON categories and NCBITaxon organisms, and tested chemicals identified by DSSTox substance IDs (DTXSID) and CAS numbers. Observations carry measurement values, detection and quantitation limits, censoring levels, lab qualifiers, and validation levels, and are attributed to the reporting organization. At roughly 53 million triples, the graph covers the national Water Quality Portal, Maine's EGAD monitoring program, EPA greenhouse gas facility reporting, and public water systems identified by PWSID. Geometries are exposed through GeoSPARQL.
