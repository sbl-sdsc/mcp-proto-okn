The Biomarker Knowledge Graph (BiomarkerKG) is a biomedical RDF knowledge graph derived from the BiomarkerKB/Clinical Biomarkers resource that organizes clinically relevant biomarkers and their relationships to diseases, molecular entities, and anatomical sources. Its schema is grounded in the Ontology for Biomarkers of Clinical Interest (OBCI). Every biomarker is typed by its BEST classification, so the graph distinguishes susceptibility/risk, diagnostic, prognostic, monitoring, predictive, and pharmacodynamic/response biomarkers as classes, and links each one to a disease or condition through a matching relationship: indicates risk of developing, diagnostic for, prognostic for, or monitors status of. Biomarkers are further connected to the assessed entity whose presence, absence, above-normal, or below-normal level indicates them, and to the anatomical structure sampled to measure them. Diseases use MONDO and Disease Ontology identifiers, assessed entities use UniProt, PubChem, and GlyTouCan, and anatomy uses UBERON. The graph answers which biomarkers indicate risk of, diagnose, or predict prognosis for a disease, which molecules underlie them, and what tissue is sampled.
