The Climate Models Knowledge Graph integrates structured information about climate models, experiments, natural hazards, and the scientific literature that describes them, supporting climate science evaluation and model development. Built for climate researchers, model developers, and policy analysts, it contains about 4.1 million triples describing roughly 362,000 entities across 51 classes. The graph centers on climate model documentation, linking 394 Sources (GCMs and RCMs) to 481 Experiments conducted by 132 Institutes and producing 3,410 climate Variables measured by 5,463 Instruments. A literature layer connects 10,910 Papers, 41,240 Authors, and 1,073 Venues to methods, problems, innovations, results, and evaluation metrics, with citation links between papers. A geographic and hazard layer spans 30,062 Cities, 252 Countries, and 3,893 subdivisions linked to GeoNames, and relates 3,546 natural hazards, 7,323 weather events, teleconnections, and ocean circulation features to the regions they affect, influence, or are observed in. Instruments, platforms, teleconnections, and ocean circulation features carry NASA GCMD keyword URIs and UUIDs.
