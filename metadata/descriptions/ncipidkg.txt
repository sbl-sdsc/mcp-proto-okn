The NCI-PID 2.0 Knowledge Graph (ncipidkg) is an RDF representation of the National Cancer Institute Pathway Interaction Database networks, a curated resource describing human molecular signaling, regulatory events, and cellular processes with strong relevance to cancer biology. It contains about 2,500 proteins identified by UniProt accession, together with chemical entities, non-coding RNAs, and gene families, connected by roughly 84,000 interactions across 207 named pathways. Biological relations use Relation Ontology predicates: molecularly interacts with, regulates, directly regulates, directly positively regulates, directly negatively regulates, participates in, and has member. Every interaction is reified as an RDF statement carrying edge-level metadata, so an assertion can be traced to its evidence count, its evidence URL, the pathway it was asserted in, and, for direct regulation, the biological process or post-translational-modification type involved, usually a Gene Ontology term. The graph supports pathway exploration, provenance-aware analysis of signaling assertions, and integration with other biomedical knowledge graphs on UniProt and ChEBI identifiers.
