The NCI-PID 2.0 Knowledge Graph (ncipidkg) is an RDF representation of the National Cancer Institute Pathway Interaction Database networks, a curated resource describing human molecular signaling and regulatory events with strong relevance to cancer biology. The release loaded in the FRINK federation contains about 2,600 molecular entities, 2,527 of them identified by UniProt accession, plus 20 gene families, connected by roughly 67,000 distinct interactions. Biological relations use Relation Ontology predicates: molecularly interacts with, regulates, directly regulates, directly positively regulates, directly negatively regulates, and has member. Every interaction is reified as an RDF statement, 83,704 in all, carrying edge-level metadata, so an assertion can be traced to its evidence count, its evidence URL, and, for direct regulation, the biological process or post-translational-modification type involved, usually a Gene Ontology term. Note that this release types every non-family entity as protein, including chemicals and non-coding RNAs, and carries no pathway layer, so pathway membership and chemical filtering are not available. Proteins join other knowledge graphs on UniProt accession.
