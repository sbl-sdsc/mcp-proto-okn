The NCI-PID 2.0 Knowledge Graph (ncipidkg) is an RDF representation of the National Cancer Institute Pathway Interaction Database networks, a curated resource describing human molecular signaling and regulatory events with strong relevance to cancer biology. The release loaded in the FRINK federation contains 2,536 proteins, nearly all identified by UniProt accession, together with 44 chemical entities, 6 non-coding RNAs and 20 gene families, connected by roughly 67,000 distinct interactions across 207 named pathways. Biological relations use Relation Ontology predicates: molecularly interacts with, regulates, directly regulates, directly positively regulates, directly negatively regulates, participates in, and has member. Every interaction is reified as an RDF statement, 83,704 in all, carrying edge-level metadata, so an assertion can be traced to its evidence count, its evidence URL, the pathways it was asserted in, and, for direct regulation, the biological process or post-translational-modification type involved, given as a Gene Ontology term. Entities are also linked directly to the pathways they participate in. Proteins join other knowledge graphs on UniProt accession, and chemicals on PubChem, ChEBI or CAS identifiers.
