The SAWGraph Spatial KG is a large-scale geospatial knowledge graph developed by the SAWGraph project (funded by NSF award #2333782) for researchers and practitioners working with place-based data and spatial analytics. It supports spatial reasoning, place-based linkage, and geographic data integration.
The graph covers 49 U.S. states and the District of Columbia (Alaska is present only as a state node, without cells or counties) and contains about 430 million triples describing 16.8 million spatial entities, including 7.4 million S2 cells (Level 13 discretization) and hierarchical administrative regions across three levels: Level 1 (50 states and the District of Columbia), Level 2 (3,114 counties), Level 3 (35,458 county subdivisions such as towns and townships). States and counties each appear under two IRIs, a KnowWhereGraph IRI and a Data Commons geoId IRI linked by owl:sameAs, so node counts for those levels are doubled (102 and 6,228). The administrative entities are hierarchically linked as well as spatially integrated with the Level 13 S2 cells using the spatial:connectedTo relation. The knowledge graph leverages the KWG ontology and implements OGC GeoSPARQL standards for interoperability with other geospatial datasets.
