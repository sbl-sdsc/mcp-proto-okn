The Rural Resilience Knowledge Graph (RuralKG) is a cross-domain semantic resource for researchers, policymakers, and public health professionals studying health disparities and justice outcomes in rural America. It integrates substance abuse data from the National Survey on Drug Use and Health (NSDUH), criminal justice variables from the National Incident-Based Reporting System (NIBRS), mental health treatment provider locations, and geospatial administrative boundaries spanning states, counties, and cities. Counties carry USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes (RUCC) to support rural-urban comparison, and are identified by FIPS and GNIS codes. The graph holds roughly 932,000 triples. The populated data covers survey questions and answer options for NSDUH and NIBRS, treatment providers and the mental health services they offer, county rurality status, and 23 substances linked to Wikidata and to the survey variables that mention them. RuralKG also declares a large NIBRS data-element and substance-abuse concept vocabulary that carries no instances in the current release; query the populated classes instead.
