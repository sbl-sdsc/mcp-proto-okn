The Rural Resilience Knowledge Graph (RuralKG) is a cross-domain semantic resource for researchers, policymakers, and public health professionals studying health disparities and justice outcomes in rural America. It integrates substance abuse data from the National Survey on Drug Use and Health (NSDUH), criminal justice variables from the National Incident-Based Reporting System (NIBRS), health survey variables from the Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS) and the Medical Expenditure Panel Survey (MEPS), mental health treatment provider locations, and geospatial administrative boundaries spanning states, counties, and cities. Counties carry USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes (RUCC) to support rural-urban comparison, and are identified by KnowWhereGraph region IRIs that embed the FIPS code, while cities use GNIS feature IRIs. The graph holds roughly 885,000 triples. The populated data covers survey questions and answer options for NSDUH, NIBRS, BRFSS and MEPS, treatment providers and the mental health services they offer, county rurality status, and 23 substances linked to Wikidata and to the survey variables that mention them. Survey variables are organised under topic classes (NIBRS data elements, substance-abuse concepts, BRFSS and MEPS sections); these classes have no instances but link to their variables through the hasVariable predicates, and NIBRS answer values carry their NIEM and NIBRS codes.
