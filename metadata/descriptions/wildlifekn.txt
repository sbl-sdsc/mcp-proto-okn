Wildlife-KN is a knowledge network for wildlife researchers, conservation managers, and policymakers addressing biodiversity challenges under climate change. The release in the FRINK federation holds about 9.4 million triples built around 420,000 observations made between 1981 and 2025. Each observation links to the organism observed, a species or genus placed in a taxonomy of about 14,600 species, 4,000 genera, 780 families, 220 orders and 36 classes, to one of 70,000 sites given by coordinates, and to its source dataset: iNaturalist occurrence records, and fish and benthic macroinvertebrate density surveys from the EPA National Rivers and Streams Assessment and USGS BioData obtained through finsyncR. The link to the organism is reified and carries the measurement type, result and unit together with the sampling method and effort. Observations also carry PRISM annual mean temperature and precipitation. Counties and states have FIPS codes, boundary polygons and KnowWhereGraph region IRIs for joining with other spatial graphs, and some species carry Wikidata and IUCN identifiers. An older layer of about 5,200 bird and amphibian sightings by named location is kept alongside.
