The Medical Device Knowledge Graph (medical-device-kg) integrates the major public FDA medical device datasets into a single RDF graph of about 17.7 million triples covering the regulatory life cycle of a device from premarket review through postmarket surveillance. Premarket submissions are represented as 175,299 510(k) clearances, 56,584 PMA approvals with 55,136 supplements, 1,947 Humanitarian Device Exemptions, and 483 De Novo authorizations, each carrying its decision, dates, applicant, and advisory committee. Postmarket data covers 39,224 recall records, 15,284 MAUDE adverse event reports, and 7,034 Total Product Life Cycle records that aggregate device problems, patient outcomes, yearly MDR trends, and recalls by product code. Supporting layers include FDA product codes and classification records, 21 CFR regulation numbers and section text, establishment registration and listing, 300,823 x-ray assembler installations, MQSA mammography facilities, 522 postmarket surveillance studies, and recognized consensus standards. Records join on FDA product code, regulation number, and manufacturer FEI number.
